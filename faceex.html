How It Works

1Face Capture

The camera captures the face and matches it with stored Aadhaar authenticated data.

2Aadhaar Authentication

The system communicates with UIDAI's secure servers to authenticate the individual's identity based on their Aadhaar data.

3Attendance Management System

The attendance is logged in real-time and stored securely for immediate access and management.

4Cloud Storage/Database

Attendance data is automatically uploaded to a cloud-based system for secure storage, analysis, and reporting.
