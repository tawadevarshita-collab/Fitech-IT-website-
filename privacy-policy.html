Your Privacy, Our Responsibility

Last Updated: October 2026

How Fi-Tech IT Solutions collects, uses, stores and protects your information across our website, mobile apps and FaceEX biometric terminals.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Fitech IT Solutions ("Company", "Fitech", "we", "our", "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our website, our companion mobile applications distributed via the Apple App Store and Google Play Store (the "Apps"), biometric hardware (FaceEX Terminal), and related software services (collectively, the "Services"). It is published in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules thereunder, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

Note on enterprise deployments: where our Services are deployed by an employer or organisation (for example, for staff attendance), that organisation is the Data Fiduciary in respect of its personnel data and determines the purposes of processing. Fitech acts as a Data Processor on its instructions. In such cases, requests to access or delete data should generally be directed to that organisation, and we will support them in fulfilling your request.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and services.

A. Personal Data: Name, email address, phone number, and professional affiliation.

B. Biometric Data and Facial Images: Through our FaceEX Terminals and, where enabled, the camera-based scanning feature within our mobile Apps, we capture facial images and process them into encrypted mathematical biometric templates used for authentication. Both the facial image captured at enrolment or scanning and the biometric template derived from it are stored securely in encrypted form and used for as long as you continue to use the Services, in order to perform authentication, re-verification and attendance matching. They are not used for any purpose other than those described in Section 3. Retention and deletion of this data are described in Sections 5 and 11.

C. Aadhaar Data: For Aadhaar-enabled services, we collect Aadhaar numbers in compliance with the Aadhaar Act, 2016 and UIDAI guidelines. This data is used strictly for authentication and is encrypted end-to-end.

D. Mobile App Device Data: The Apps may request access to your device's camera (to capture facial images for biometric scanning as described above), storage (to process and securely hold scan data on your device where required for the App to function), and notification permissions (to deliver attendance and authentication alerts). You control these permissions through your device settings at any time; declining a permission may limit related App functionality. The Apps may also collect basic device diagnostics (device model, OS version, crash logs) to maintain reliability.

E. Website Camera Use: Our public website (fitechitsol.in) does not request or access your device's camera. The animated visuals on our site are pre-rendered graphics, not a live camera feed.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

  • To facilitate account creation and logon process.
  • To provide biometric authentication and attendance services.
  • To manage user accounts and provide technical support.
  • To respond to enquiries submitted through our website contact form.
  • To comply with legal obligations and government regulations.
  • To improve our hardware and software performance through anonymized analytics.

4. CONSENT AND WITHDRAWAL OF CONSENT

Where required by law, we process your personal and biometric data only on the basis of your free, informed, specific and unambiguous consent, obtained at the time of enrolment or through in-App prompts before any camera or biometric feature is used. Aadhaar-based authentication is performed only with the explicit consent required under the Aadhaar Act, 2016 and UIDAI regulations.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer (Section 13) or, where applicable, through your employer or the organisation that enrolled you. Withdrawal is as easy as giving consent. Withdrawing consent will end further processing but does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, and may mean you can no longer use biometric authentication or related features of the Services.

5. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal data only for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this Policy, and in accordance with the retention obligations imposed by applicable Indian law and regulatory compliance requirements.

A. Account and App Data: Retained for as long as you continue to use the Services, and deleted when you delete your account or request deletion, subject to Section 5.D below.

B. Facial Images and Biometric Templates: Retained in encrypted form for as long as you continue to use the Services and your enrolment remains active, as they are required for ongoing authentication and re-verification. They are deleted upon deletion of your account, upon withdrawal of consent, or upon termination or expiry of the enterprise deployment under which you were enrolled, whichever occurs first.

C. Aadhaar-Related Data and Authentication Logs: Retained, archived and deleted strictly in accordance with the retention periods mandated by the Aadhaar Act, 2016, the regulations, circulars and directions issued by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI), and any other applicable law. Where the law mandates a minimum retention period, we retain such records for that period and no longer than permitted.

D. Statutory and Legal Retention: Notwithstanding the above, we may retain certain records for longer where required to comply with applicable law (including taxation, corporate record-keeping, and information technology regulations), to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, or to comply with a lawful direction of a court, regulator or government authority. Such retained data is kept only for the period required and is protected by the safeguards described in Section 6.

E. Contact Form Submissions: Retained for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and to maintain ordinary business records, after which they are deleted.

Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, data is securely deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

6. DATA SECURITY

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures. For our biometric systems:

  • Facial images, biometric templates and other sensitive data are encrypted at rest using industry-standard protocols (AES-256).
  • Data in transit is protected using TLS encryption.
  • Transmission to UIDAI or central servers occurs via secure, private GSM or dedicated networks.
  • We follow the principle of "Data Minimization," storing only what is strictly necessary for service delivery.
  • Access to personal and biometric data is restricted to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and while we strive to protect your data using commercially acceptable means, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and each affected Data Principal without undue delay, in the form and manner prescribed under the DPDP Act, 2023 and applicable rules, and will report to CERT-In where required under the Information Technology Act, 2000. Notifications will describe the nature of the breach, its likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to mitigate it.

8. DISCLOSURE OF DATA

We only share information with your consent, to comply with laws, to provide you with services, or to protect your rights. This includes sharing with:

  • Authorized government bodies (e.g., UIDAI) for biometric verification.
  • Enterprise clients who have deployed our systems for their internal attendance/security.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under written contracts imposing confidentiality and data protection obligations no less protective than this Policy.
  • Courts, regulators or law enforcement where required by a valid legal process.

We do not sell your personal data, biometric data or device data to any third party.

9. DATA LOCALISATION AND CROSS-BORDER TRANSFERS

Facial images, biometric templates, Aadhaar-related data and the account data associated with our Apps and FaceEX Terminals are stored and processed on infrastructure located within India, in accordance with the data localisation requirements of the Aadhaar Act, 2016 and the regulations and directions issued by UIDAI. This data is not transferred outside India.

Enquiries submitted through the contact form on our public website are processed on our web hosting infrastructure. Where any such data is stored outside India, the transfer is carried out in accordance with Section 16 of the DPDP Act, 2023 and is subject to contractual safeguards requiring a standard of protection consistent with this Policy. We do not transfer personal data to any territory restricted by the Central Government.

10. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Subject to applicable law, including the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and a summary of processing.
  • Request the correction, completion or updating of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request erasure of your personal data (subject to Section 5.D legal retention requirements).
  • Withdraw consent for biometric or Aadhaar processing, as described in Section 4.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Revoke camera, storage, or notification permissions granted to the Apps at any time via your device settings.
  • Register a grievance with our Grievance Officer (Section 13), and to escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

11. ACCOUNT AND DATA DELETION

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time:

  • In the Apps: use the account deletion option provided within the App and confirm the request.
  • By email: send a deletion request to d.ghosh@fitechitsol.in from your registered email address, stating the account or enrolment you wish to have deleted.
  • By phone or post: using the Grievance Officer contact details in Section 13.
  • Enterprise users: if your account was created by your employer or another organisation, please direct your request to that organisation, which controls the deployment; we will assist them in giving effect to it.

What is deleted: your profile and account data, your stored facial images and biometric templates, device identifiers, and App usage data associated with your account.

What may be retained: records we are required by law to keep, as described in Section 5.D, and anonymised data that can no longer identify you.

Timeline: verified deletion requests are actioned within 30 days of receipt, and we will confirm to you once completed.

12. COOKIES AND LOCAL STORAGE

Our website uses browser local storage strictly for essential functionality, such as remembering your light or dark theme preference. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or third-party behavioural advertising trackers on our website. You may clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

15. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The Apps and Services are not directed to children under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal or biometric data from children through our public website or consumer App downloads. Where our Services are deployed by an educational institution or other organisation whose members may include minors, such processing is carried out solely on the instructions of that institution as Data Fiduciary, which is responsible for obtaining verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian as required by Section 9 of the DPDP Act, 2023. We do not undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children. If you believe a child's data has been provided to us without appropriate consent, contact our Grievance Officer and we will delete it promptly.

When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Policy. Where changes are material, we will provide prominent notice through the Services or the Apps, and where required by law, seek your fresh consent. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically. Continued use of the Services after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
